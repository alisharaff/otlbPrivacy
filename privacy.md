# Information We Collect
# 1. Account & Profile Information

To access app features, users (clients and technicians/service providers) may need to register or log in using:

Full name

Phone number

Email address

Password

This information is used strictly for:

User authentication and identity verification

Account management and security

Communication regarding service requests and updates

We do not sell or rent your personal login information to third parties.

# 2. Service Requests & Location Details

To connect clients with nearby technicians and complete service orders, we may collect:

Service addresses and GPS/approximate location data (to assign nearby service providers)

Details of the requested job (category, description, preferred time)

Photos or media uploaded to illustrate the issue or maintenance required

Direct feedback, ratings, and reviews submitted after service completion

# 3. Advertising ID and Analytics

OtlobSanaye may use device identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID (AAID), to:

Monitor application stability and performance

Enhance overall user experience

Deliver relevant promotions or updates (if applicable)

The Advertising ID does not personally identify you. You can reset or restrict ad tracking at any time through your Android device settings.

# How We Use Your Information
We process your data to:

Facilitate direct communication between clients and service providers regarding bookings

Provide customer support and troubleshoot technical issues

Prevent fraudulent activities and ensure platform safety
